01 — The team

MADE BY
STUDENTS.

Chitrak is a student-led electric motorcycle programme at RV College of Engineering. The team designs, fabricates, tests and presents the machine — and every part of the programme is run by students.

03

Privacy — data use

YOUR DATA,
EXPLAINED.

Team Chitrak RVCE collects the information in this form solely to review recruitment registrations and contact applicants about their registration.

  • Who can access it: authorised Team Chitrak reviewers only. Applicant data is not published or shared publicly.
  • Where it is stored: in a private recruitment spreadsheet controlled by the team. It is not stored in your browser beyond this session.
  • How long it is kept: registration records are scheduled for deletion after 180 days unless a longer period is needed for an active recruitment process.
  • Your choices: you can request access, correction or deletion of your registration by emailing chitrak@rvce.edu.in with your submission ID.

Privacy notice — v1. Do not submit information you are not comfortable sharing for recruitment purposes.
